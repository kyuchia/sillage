SILLAGE

Montréal's transit network, drawn through the trails its vehicles leave behind.

Sillage brings together more than 7.6 million recorded positions, tracking buses and aircraft at 20-second intervals since April 2026, alongside schedule-simulated métro, REM, and commuter rail services.

Colour distinguishes each mode of transport, drawing from the official palettes of Montréal's transit operators, while brightness reflects vehicle speed. This site presents a series of curated scenes rendered from the recorded data. Run the project locally to query the full archive across arbitrary time windows.

Sonification is currently in development.
